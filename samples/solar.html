Questions before you commit

Is the rebate really still available?

Yes. The federal STC rebate applies in 2026 and reduces each year. We apply it as an upfront discount, so the price you're quoted is the price you pay.

What if my roof isn't suitable?

We check tilt, orientation and shading from satellite imagery before quoting. If it doesn't stack up, we tell you — there's no point installing a system that won't pay back.

Do I need a battery?

Usually not at first. Batteries make sense above roughly $1,000 a quarter or when you're home during the day. We'll say so honestly on the call.

How long does approval take?

Grid approval is typically 5–10 business days, and we manage the whole application for you.
