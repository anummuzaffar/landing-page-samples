Common questions

Does it hurt?

Placement is done under local anaesthetic and most patients report less discomfort than an extraction. Sleep dentistry is available if you're anxious.

How long does it take?

Placement is about an hour per implant. Healing takes 3–4 months, and you wear a temporary tooth throughout — you're never without one.

Am I a candidate if I've lost bone?

Usually yes. The 3D scan tells us immediately whether grafting is needed, and that's included in the written quote.

Is the consult really free?

Yes — consult and 3D scan, normally $250, at no cost and no obligation.
